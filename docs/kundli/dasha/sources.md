# Classical Scriptural Sources & Scholarly Literature

## 1. Primary Foundational Texts

### 1. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS)
- **Author**: Maharishi Parashara
- **Key Chapters**:
  - *Adhyaya 46*: Dasha Adhyaya (दशाऽध्यायः) — Complete exposition of the 120-year Vimshottari cycle, lord durations, Nakshatra rulerships, and calculation of Bhukta/Bhogya balance.
  - *Adhyaya 47*: Antardasha Adhyaya (अन्तर्दशाऽध्यायः) — Sub-period proportions, calculation of 9 Antardashas per Mahadasha.
  - *Adhyaya 48–50*: Pratyantardasha, Sookshma, and Prana Dashas; delineation of dasha phala (effects).
  - *Adhyaya 51–53*: Ashtottari, Shodashottari, Dvadashottari, and Yogini Dashas.

### 2. Phaladeepika
- **Author**: Mantreswara (13th Century CE)
- **Key Chapter**: *Adhyaya 19–20* (दशाफलनिर्णयः)
  - Detailed rules for the calculation of Vimshottari periods.
  - Rules for fractional month and day allocations ($Y \times 12, M \times 30$).

### 3. Saravali
- **Author**: Kalyana Varma (8th Century CE)
- **Key Chapter**: *Adhyaya 41* (दशाऽन्तर्दशा निरूपणम्)
  - Systematic principles for analyzing Mahadashas and sub-periods.

### 4. Jaimini Upadesha Sutras
- **Author**: Maharishi Jaimini
- **Key Discourse**: *Adhyayas 1–2*
  - Foundation of Rashi-based Dasha systems: Chara Dasha (चर दशा), Sthira Dasha, and Manduka Dasha.

## 2. Modern Computational Astronomy Literature

1. **Dr. B.V. Raman** — *Planetary Influences on Human Affairs* and *Studies in Jaimini Astrology*. (Exposition of Savana year vs 365.25-day year conventions).
2. **K.N. Rao** — *Predicting Through Vimshottari Dasha*. (Practical rules on Pratyantardasha and Sookshma dasha timing).
3. **P.V.R. Narasimha Rao** — *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*. (Algorithms for micro-tier division and astronomical length of solar years).
