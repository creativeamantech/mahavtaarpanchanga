# Cheshta Bala (Motional Strength) — Mathematical Specification

## 1. Classical Basis
BPHS Adhyaya 27, Shlokas 23–27 defines Cheshta Bala (चेष्टाबल) as the motional strength acquired by planets through variations in orbital velocity, stationary turns, and retrograde movement relative to the Sun.

## 2. Tara Grahas (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn)
For the five planets with noticeable orbital anomalies, BPHS recognizes 8 classical motional states (Gatis):
1. **Vakra (Retrograde)**: Planet is moving backward in celestial longitude. Strength = **60.0 Virupas**.
2. **Anuvakra (Direct after Retrograde / Resuming direct motion)**: Strength = **30.0 Virupas**.
3. **Vikala (Stationary / Near-zero daily speed, within $\pm 0.05^\circ/\text{day}$)**: Strength = **15.0 Virupas**.
4. **Manda (Slow direct motion, $< 50\%$ of mean speed)**: Strength = **30.0 Virupas**.
5. **Mandatara (Extremely slow direct motion, $< 25\%$ of mean speed)**: Strength = **15.0 Virupas**.
6. **Sama (Mean daily speed, $\pm 20\%$ of mean speed)**: Strength = **7.5 Virupas**.
7. **Chara (Accelerated direct motion, $> 120\%$ of mean speed)**: Strength = **45.0 Virupas**.
8. **Atichara (Super-accelerated motion, $> 150\%$ of mean speed)**: Strength = **30.0 Virupas**.

### Canonical Mean Daily Speeds (Deg / Day):
- Mars: $0.5240^\circ$
- Mercury: $1.3833^\circ$
- Jupiter: $0.0831^\circ$
- Venus: $1.2000^\circ$
- Saturn: $0.0335^\circ$

## 3. Special Rule for Luminaries (Sun & Moon)
The Sun and Moon never undergo retrograde motion. Classical authorities (BPHS Ch. 27 v. 27) prescribe:
- **Sun's Cheshta Bala**: Substituted by the Sun's **Ayana Bala**.
- **Moon's Cheshta Bala**: Substituted by the Moon's **Paksha Bala**.

## 4. Nodes (Rahu & Ketu)
Rahu and Ketu are mathematical intersection nodes without physical masses. Standard Parashari Shadbala applies strictly to the seven classical Grahas (Saptagrahas). When nodes are included in extended variants, they inherit fixed mean retrograde values.
