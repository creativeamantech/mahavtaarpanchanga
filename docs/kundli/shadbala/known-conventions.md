# Known Conventions and Variations in Classical Shadbala

## 1. Saptavargaja Bala Scoring Differences
- **BPHS Standard**:
  - Moolatrikona: 45
  - Swakshetra: 30
  - Adhi-Mitra: 20
  - Mitra: 15
  - Sama: 10
  - Shatru: 5
  - Adhi-Shatru: 2.5
- **Alternative / B. V. Raman Variant**:
  - Moolatrikona: 45
  - Swakshetra: 30
  - Adhi-Mitra: 22.5
  - Mitra: 15
  - Sama: 7.5
  - Shatru: 3.75
  - Adhi-Shatru: 1.875
- **Selected Convention in Mahavtaar**: Canonical BPHS standard (45 / 30 / 20 / 15 / 10 / 5 / 2.5) with configurable Raman scaling support.

## 2. Mercury's Natonnata Bala
- **Convention A (BPHS Literal)**: Mercury gets 60 Virupas at all times because of its dual nature as both diurnal and nocturnal.
- **Convention B (Variable)**: Mercury varies based on daytime/nighttime birth like other benefics.
- **Selected Convention**: Convention A (60 Virupas fixed constant for Natonnata).

## 3. Moon's Paksha Bala
- **Convention A (Doubled)**: Moon's Paksha Bala is multiplied by 2 because the Moon's very nature is tied to the lunar fortnight (BPHS Ch. 27 v. 18).
- **Convention B (Single)**: Moon receives single Paksha Bala like other benefics/malefics.
- **Selected Convention**: Convention A (doubled for Moon).

## 4. Cheshta Bala for Sun and Moon
- Since the Sun and Moon are never retrograde, classical authorities do not compute orbital retrogradation for them:
  - Sun Cheshta Bala = Sun's Ayana Bala (BPHS Ch. 27 v. 27).
  - Moon Cheshta Bala = Moon's Paksha Bala (BPHS Ch. 27 v. 27).
- Both conventions are explicitly implemented.
