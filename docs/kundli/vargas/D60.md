# D60 — Shashtiamsha Chart (षष्ट्यंश चक्र)

## 1. Purpose & Scope
The supreme micro-divisional chart in Vedic astrology. BPHS verse 6.42 declares: *"षष्ट्यंशे सकलं ज्ञेयम्"* ("Everything must be judged from the Shashtiamsha"). It holds the highest weighting in Vimshopaka Bala (5 points out of 20, greater than D1 at 3.5 and D9 at 3.0). It reveals subtle past-life karma (*sanchita karma*), root causes of inexplicable fortunes/misfortunes, and micro-destiny.

## 2. Classical Source
- **Text**: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS), Chapter 6, Verses 33-41.
- **Verse Excerpt**:
  > *षष्ट्यंशस्य च गणना तत्तद्राशेः प्रजायते।*
  > "The counting of the Shashtiamsha always begins from the sign itself."

## 3. Calculation Convention
- Each rashi of $30^\circ$ is divided into 60 equal parts of $0^\circ 30'$ ($0.5^\circ$) each.
- In every sign, the counting starts from the sign itself:
  $$\text{D60 Sign} = (\text{signIndex} + \text{part}) \pmod{12}$$
- **The 60 Classical Deities & Natures**:
  Parashara lists 60 specific deities. In odd signs, they are counted 1 to 60. In even signs, they are counted 60 to 1 (reversed) or in traditional cyclic order. Each Shashtiamsha is classified as **Benefic (शुभ)** or **Malefic (क्रूर / अशुभ)**:

## 7. Known Ambiguities
- **Deity Counting in Even Signs**: Some traditions count the deities 1-60 directly in all signs, while others (following specific commentaries on BPHS) reverse the deity order for even signs (60 down to 1). Our canonical engine documents both and supports standard BPHS direct/reversed mapping while sign calculation remains universally $\text{sign} + \text{part} \pmod{12}$.
